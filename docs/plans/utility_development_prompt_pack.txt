Prompt 1: Desktop Inventory
Use $multi-platform-team-workflow and $shared-contract-governance.

Spawn repository_analyzer, solution_architect, integration_tester, security_reviewer, and release_engineer.
Inventory the desktop utility system and identify every module for:
- consumer management
- new connection
- rates
- scheduling
- billing
- bill updates
- bill adjustments
- collections
- disconnection
- reconnection
- lifeline
- senior
- net metering
- reports
- audit
- reconciliation
- optional accounting integration

Return:
- desktop module list
- functions per module
- tables touched
- fields required
- fields that differ between account families
- data risks and contract drift risks
